8. Your rights as a data subject

Under the GDPR, you have the following rights:

  • Right of access (Art. 15 GDPR)

  • Right to restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR)
  • Right to data portability (Art. 20 GDPR)

To exercise these rights, an informal message to the contact address above is sufficient.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR). The authority responsible for the controller's place of business is the Data Protection Authority of Lower Saxony (Landesbeauftragte für den Datenschutz Niedersachsen, LfD Niedersachsen).
